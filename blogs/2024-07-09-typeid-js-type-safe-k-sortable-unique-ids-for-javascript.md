Since we first announced TypeID last year, we've seen significant adoption and interest from the community, with 23 different language clients contributed by the community and 90,000 weekly NPM downloads of our Typescript Implementation. Last week, we released version 1.0 of our Typescript implementation, TypeID-JS . To celebrate this release, we wanted to share more about why we wrote TypeID, and how we use it to ensure type safety at Jetify.
