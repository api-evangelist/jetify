With Devbox 0.4.3, we've fully shifted our environment generation from using standard Nix expressions to using Nix Flakes, a new experimental feature in the Nix ecosystem. While this is mostly a behind the scenes change to Devbox, we're excited about the potential this unlocks, and we wanted to share why we made this shift. In this post we'll describe why Flakes are so important, and the benefits our users should see from this change.
