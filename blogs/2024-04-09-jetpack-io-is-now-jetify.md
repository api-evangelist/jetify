Today, I want to share some special news about our company: jetpack.io is now Jetify. Our primary website will now live at jetify.com and our branding will be updated to use the name Jetify . Our mission We started this company with a clear goal: to help developers build cloud applications in ways that are faster, easier, and more reliable than ever before while removing boilerplate.
